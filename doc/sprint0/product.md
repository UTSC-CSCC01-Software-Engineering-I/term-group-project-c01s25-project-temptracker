## **Description**

Our product is the tempTracker web application, which allows users to upload location-based water data. It uses this data to create a colour-coded temperature map and to show various analytical information. Its users can be anyone as long as they have a device that can connect to the internet. But it is mainly targeted at people who love water sports or going to the beach. As well as scientists who need to use water temperature in their research. For our feature prioritization, we will first focus on the main functionality of our product, then on the analysis part, and finally on gamification. 

According to my research, a quote from https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/news/2019/06/canada-and-the-united-states-release-progress-report-showing-continuing-restoration-of-the-great-lakes.html, says that “40 million people live near the great lakes, which is 1 in 10 Americans and 1 in 3 Canadians.” This is a sizeable amount and can be helpful to certain users and help them make informed decisions about their water behaviour (recreational, personal or travel).

Priority 1: 

- User authentication feature (login, register, forget password)
- Upload water temperature data as JSON
- Be able to view a colour-coded water temperature map
- Be able to look at water temperature archives (compare with past day, month, etc)

Priority 2:

- Fahrenheit and Celsius toggle to accommodate all users
- Interactive home page
- Verify inputted data using confidence levels
- Look at historical water data
- Web application optimized for mobile devices
- Add settings for customization and increased UX (exact settings TBD later)

Priority 3:

- Show the most popular lakes
- Expand to other lakes in Ontario (Lake Simcoe, Kawartha Lakes)
- Show local weather as well for user convenience
- A points system to encourage data input
- Gamification (badges, leaderboard, experimental)
- Be able to enter JSON or CSV files
